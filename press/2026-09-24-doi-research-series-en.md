# Low Season Growth publishes four hospitality research datasets with persistent DOI records

**24 September 2026**

Low Season Growth has published four open hospitality research datasets with persistent DOI identifiers through Zenodo.

The series covers European tourism seasonality, international versus domestic demand in Europe, Caribbean low-season RevPAR and source-market diversification, and Japan inbound tourism and source-market divergence.

## DOI registry

- Research Note 01 — European Tourism Seasonality 2026  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22937580

- Research Note 02 — International Tourism Demand in Europe 2026  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22938213

- Research Note 03 — Caribbean Low Season 2026  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22938535

- Research Note 04 — Japan Tourism 2026  
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22938971

The DOI records provide persistent identifiers for the archived datasets and supporting materials. They do not imply peer review or endorsement by the original source institutions.

Low Season Growth distinguishes official source figures from LSG descriptive calculations and publishes source references, methodology notes and downloadable data.
