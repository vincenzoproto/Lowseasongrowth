# Press Release — Low Season Growth Expands International Hospitality Outreach

**Release date:** 24 September 2026  
**Website:** https://lowseasongrowth.com

Low Season Growth (LSG), a hospitality commercial-strategy initiative focused on weaker-demand periods, is expanding its international outreach across Europe, North America, Japan, South Korea, China, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East.

LSG is built around a simple operating principle: low season should be treated as a distinct commercial challenge, not as a generic marketing problem. Instead of relying only on broad discounts or increased OTA dependence, the approach starts from the property's weakest dates and builds focused commercial action around those gaps.

Its core commercial offer is the **30-Day Booking Sprint**, a focused engagement designed for independent hotels, boutique properties, B&Bs and other accommodation businesses. The Sprint combines positioning, targeted outreach, direct-booking opportunities, partnership development and practical sales actions around specific periods that need stronger demand.

Low Season Growth is now extending its content, research, media and partner outreach internationally, with dedicated attention to local hospitality markets and languages rather than using a one-size-fits-all approach.

The broader objective is to build a cross-market knowledge and distribution network around low-season hospitality growth, connecting practical commercial execution with local demand patterns, industry media, hospitality professionals and independent operators.

> **Hospitality for a brighter tomorrow.**

Editors, hospitality professionals and industry partners interested in low-season demand, independent hotel growth, direct-booking strategy or commercial collaboration can contact Low Season Growth through its official website.
