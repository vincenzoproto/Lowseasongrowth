# Low Season Growth Opens LSG Intelligence Beta for Hotel Weak-Date Decision Support

**2 October 2026**

Low Season Growth has opened the public beta of **LSG Intelligence**, a decision-support system for independent hotels, B&Bs and guesthouses working on specific weak dates and low-demand periods.

The beta is built around a narrow commercial question: when a defined stay period is underperforming, what should the property investigate before choosing a discount, campaign, source market or channel action?

LSG Intelligence is designed as a decision-support layer rather than another hotel operating system. It does not replace a PMS, RMS, channel manager, booking engine or commercial team, and it does not promise bookings or revenue outcomes.

The public beta documents the product scope and selected decision-support concepts. Proprietary weighting, internal learning systems, CRM data and private operating rules are not published.

## About Low Season Growth

Low Season Growth is a hospitality commercial-strategy and research initiative focused on weak-demand periods for independent hotels, B&Bs and guesthouses. Its public work includes source-backed hospitality research, practical low-season frameworks and decision-support tools.
