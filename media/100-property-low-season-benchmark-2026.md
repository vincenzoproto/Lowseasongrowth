# 100-Property Low Season Benchmark 2026

**Low Season Growth** has opened a research call for independent hotels, B&Bs, guesthouses and hospitality professionals to contribute to the **100-Property Low Season Benchmark 2026**.

The benchmark is designed to collect comparable signals on:

- the months and recurring periods that are hardest to fill;
- which acquisition and sales channels perform outside peak season;
- which actions have generated measurable booking movement;
- where independent properties believe commercial opportunities are most often lost.

Participation can be anonymous or publicly credited. There is no participation fee and no commercial obligation.

## Why this project exists

Low-season performance is often discussed through broad destination statistics. The benchmark is intended to add a property-level view: what independent accommodation operators actually see during weak periods and which commercial responses they consider useful.

Results will be reported in aggregate where appropriate. Low Season Growth does not publish private CRM data or confidential customer information in its public research repository.
