# Low Season Growth Weak-Date Vocabulary

Public working vocabulary used across Low Season Growth research, guides and decision-support tools.

Canonical glossary:

Canonical method registry:

## Core terms

### Weak date
A specific stay date, weekday pattern, week or short period that is materially softer than the property expects or needs.

### Weak-date cluster
A recurring group of soft stay dates that share a similar pattern and can be diagnosed together.

### Booking window
The time between reservation creation and arrival.

### Booking pace
The rate at which reservations accumulate for a defined stay period.

### Baseline
The expected outcome without the new commercial activity, based on stated assumptions and available evidence.

### Target
A chosen commercial objective. A target is not a forecast or guaranteed outcome.

### Demand problem
Too few relevant guests have a credible reason to travel on the target dates.

### Fit / positioning problem
Relevant demand may exist, but the property, offer or message does not match the intended travel need strongly enough.

### Distribution problem
The right proposition may exist, but the relevant audience is not encountering it through effective channels or routes.

### Conversion problem
Interest exists but does not become completed business because of friction in offer clarity, availability, booking, terms, response or trust.

### Direct-booking path
The complete sequence from discovery to completed direct reservation.

### Commercial signal
An intermediate observable response such as a qualified reply, proposal request or direct enquiry. A signal is not the same as a completed booking.

### Action test
A limited commercial intervention with a defined hypothesis, audience, offer, channel, review point and stop conditions.
