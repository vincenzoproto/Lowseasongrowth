# LSG Intelligence Beta — Hotel Weak-Date Decision Support

**LSG Intelligence** is a beta decision-support system from **Low Season Growth** for independent hotels, B&Bs and guesthouses working on specific weak dates and low-demand periods.

Canonical public beta:  
https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/lsg-intelligence

Beta introduction:  
https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/lsg-intelligence-beta-hotel-weak-dates

## Purpose

The beta is designed around a narrow commercial question: when a defined hotel period is underperforming, what should be tested first?

It is intended to help structure a weak-date problem before a property chooses discounts, campaigns, channel actions or other commercial tactics.

## Scope

LSG Intelligence is a decision-support layer. It is not a PMS, RMS, channel manager, booking engine, revenue forecast or guarantee of bookings.

The public beta documents the product scope and selected methodology concepts. Proprietary decision rules, weighting logic, internal learning systems, CRM data and operational intelligence are not published in this repository.
