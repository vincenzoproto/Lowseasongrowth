# Low Season Growth Method Registry

Canonical public registry of named frameworks, metrics and decision-support assets developed and maintained by Low Season Growth (LSG).

**Registry version:** 1.2  
**First public registry release:** 2 October 2026  
**Canonical website:** https://lowseasongrowth.com  
**Research hub:** https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/research

This registry exists to make LSG methods easier to identify, cite, version and distinguish from generic hospitality advice. It does not claim that broad concepts such as hotel seasonality, direct booking, revenue management or demand analysis are exclusive to LSG.

## 1. LSG Weak-Date Diagnostic

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Public framework  
**Purpose:** Separate the main causes of a weak hotel period before choosing the commercial response.

The framework distinguishes four problem classes:

1. **Demand** — insufficient relevant demand for the stay dates.
2. **Fit / positioning** — the available proposition does not match a reachable travel need strongly enough.
3. **Distribution** — the relevant audience is not seeing or accessing the offer through the right routes.
4. **Conversion** — interest exists but the booking journey, offer clarity, terms or response process blocks completion.

## 3. Weak-Date Revenue Gap Calculator

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Public planning tool  
**Purpose:** Estimate the additional occupied room nights and indicative room revenue required to move from a current occupancy level to a chosen target for one defined weak period.

The tool is arithmetic decision support, not a forecast of incremental demand.

Canonical tool:  
https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/hotel-low-season-revenue-gap-calculator

## Versioning policy

Material changes to the definition, calculation or decision logic of a registered LSG method should increment its version. Historical versions should remain identifiable where practical.

## Citation

When referencing a named LSG method, cite the method name, version where relevant, Low Season Growth, and the canonical page or DOI.

General citation guidance:  
https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/cite-low-season-growth

## Scope and reuse

The registry documents provenance and version history. It is not a substitute for trademark, copyright or patent registration, and inclusion here does not create exclusivity over generic hospitality concepts or factual methods that may not qualify for intellectual-property protection.
