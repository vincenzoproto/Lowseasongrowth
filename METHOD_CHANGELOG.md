# LSG Method Changelog

Public change history for named Low Season Growth frameworks, metrics and decision-support assets.

## 2026-10-02 — Registry v1.1

- Added **LSG Weak-Date Case Study Protocol v1.0**.
- Added a public JSON Schema for consistent weak-date case-study records.
- Connected the protocol to the public method registry, Research Hub and Resources Hub.
- Clarified the public/private boundary: measurement structure is public; property-level operational data and accumulated learning data remain private.

## 2026-10-02 — Registry v1.0

- Published the first public **LSG Method Registry**.
- Registered **LSG Weak-Date Diagnostic v1.0** with four problem classes: demand, fit/positioning, distribution and conversion.
- Registered **LSG Peak-to-Trough Concentration Gap v1.0** as a descriptive LSG metric.
- Registered **Weak-Date Revenue Gap Calculator v1.0** as a planning tool, not a demand forecast.
- Registered **LSG Lift v0.1** as a framework in development.
- Registered **LSG Intelligence Beta 0.1** as the public decision-support layer, while keeping internal models and operational intelligence private.
- Registered the **100-Property Low Season Benchmark 2026** as an open research project in recruitment.

## Versioning rules

- Major conceptual changes increment the major version.
- Material changes to definitions or decision logic increment the minor version.
- Editorial clarifications that do not change the underlying method can be recorded without changing the method version.
- Historical versions should remain identifiable where practical.

## Scope

This changelog records public provenance and evolution. It does not claim exclusivity over generic hospitality concepts and is not a substitute for formal intellectual-property registration.
