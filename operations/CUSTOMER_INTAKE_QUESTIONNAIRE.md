# LowSeasonGrowth — Customer Intake Questionnaire

## Purpose
This questionnaire starts immediately after purchase and gives the operations team the minimum information needed to produce a standardized LowSeasonGrowth deliverable.

## Customer-facing introduction
Thank you for choosing LowSeasonGrowth. Complete this form so we can build your low-season growth plan around your property, your weak dates and your commercial goals.

Estimated completion time: 8–12 minutes.

---

## Section 1 — Property
1. Property / brand name
2. Country
3. City / destination
4. Property type
   - B&B
   - Vacation rental
   - Airbnb / short-term rental
   - Boutique hotel
   - Guesthouse
   - Other
5. Number of rooms / units
6. Website URL
7. Airbnb URL
8. Booking.com / OTA URL
9. Instagram / social URL

## Section 2 — Current performance
10. Average nightly rate / ADR
11. Approximate average occupancy
12. Strongest months
13. Weakest months
14. Specific weak dates or periods you want to improve
15. Average length of stay
16. Main booking channels
   - Direct website
   - Airbnb
   - Booking.com
   - Other OTA
   - Travel agents
   - Social / direct messages
17. Approximate percentage of direct bookings, if known

## Section 3 — Customer and positioning
18. Main guest segments today
19. Guest segments you would like to attract
20. Top 3 reasons guests choose your property
21. Main booking objections or recurring questions
22. What makes the property different from nearby alternatives?

## Section 4 — Pricing and offers
23. Do you currently use seasonal pricing?
24. Do you currently use last-minute discounts?
25. Do you offer minimum-stay or length-of-stay promotions?
26. Do you have direct-booking benefits?
27. Which offers or promotions have you already tested?
28. Which offers performed best / worst?

## Section 5 — Marketing
29. Current marketing activities
30. Do you run paid ads? If yes, where and approximate monthly spend?
31. How often do you publish on social media?
32. Do you have professional photos / video?
33. Do you use email marketing or a guest database?
34. Have you worked with creators / influencers / local partners?

## Section 6 — Competitive set
35. Name or link 3–5 properties you consider direct competitors
36. Are there competitors that seem to perform better in low season? Why?

## Section 7 — Objectives
37. Main objective for the next 90 days
   - Increase occupancy
   - Fill specific dates
   - Increase ADR
   - Increase direct bookings
   - Reduce OTA dependency
   - Improve listing conversion
   - Build a repeatable low-season strategy
38. Target occupancy for the weak period
39. Target revenue increase, if defined
40. Marketing budget available for the next 90 days
41. Anything else we should know?

---

## Internal fields — not shown to customer
- Order ID
- Package purchased
- Market / language
- Assigned operator
- Intake received date
- Delivery due date
- QA owner
- Upsell eligibility
- Status: Waiting intake / Research / Draft / QA / Delivered / Upsell

## Automation rule
After successful payment:
1. Send confirmation email.
2. Send unique intake questionnaire link.
3. Create order record in operations tracker.
4. If no submission after 24h: reminder #1.
5. If no submission after 72h: reminder #2.
6. When submitted: change status to `Research` and assign operator.
