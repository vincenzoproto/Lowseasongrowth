# LSG Weak-Date Case Study Protocol

**Version:** 1.0  
**Published:** 2 October 2026  
**Canonical page:** https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/lsg-weak-date-case-study-protocol

The LSG Weak-Date Case Study Protocol defines the minimum structure Low Season Growth uses to measure and report commercial work around a specific weak hotel period.

Its purpose is comparability, transparent reporting and longitudinal learning.

## Required fields

1. Exact stay period.
2. Measurement / extraction date.
3. Eligible available room nights.
4. Occupied room nights at baseline.
5. Baseline assumption.
6. Selected target.
7. Primary weak-date diagnosis.
8. Audience or segment tested.
9. Offer or proposition tested.
10. Channels and actions used.
11. Qualified commercial signals.
12. Confirmed bookings and room nights.
13. Cancellations.
14. Completed room nights.
15. Realized room revenue when authorized and available.
16. Relevant incremental costs.
17. Measurement status: provisional or mature.
18. Limitations and confounding changes.

## Measurement principles

- Separate baseline from target.
- Separate signals from final outcomes.
- Reconcile final business with the property reservation system where possible.
- Do not double-count platform-reported conversions.
- Distinguish confirmed, cancelled and completed outcomes.
- State the measurement date and stay-period maturity.
- Record material price, inventory, restriction and channel changes.
- Use descriptive attribution language when causal lift cannot be established credibly.
- Keep guest identities and private CRM information out of public records.
- Publish a named case study only after property approval.

## Public vs private data

This protocol is public.

Property-level operational data, guest data, CRM records, private commercial terms, internal learning features and the accumulated LSG Intelligence training dataset remain private unless explicitly approved for release.
