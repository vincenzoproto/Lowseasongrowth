## Primary customer

Independent B&Bs, guest houses and small hospitality properties with identifiable low-season demand gaps and limited internal marketing capacity.

## Offer ladder

| Offer | Price | Primary job |
|---|---:|---|
| Revenue Audit | €249 | Diagnose the biggest low-season commercial gaps |

| Growth Partner | €990 | Build the full campaign and guide implementation for 30 days |

The Revenue Audit is the entry offer. Its €249 fee is credited toward a Booking Sprint or Growth Partner upgrade made within 14 days of delivery. The Booking Sprint is the main offer. Growth Partner should normally be sold after a short fit conversation.

## 14-day revenue sprint

Target: close one Revenue Audit first, then convert one qualified client to the Booking Sprint.

Daily actions:

1. Select 10 independent properties with visible low-season or direct-booking gaps.
2. Record one specific observation for each property.
3. Send a personalised message offering three observations—not a generic sales pitch.
4. Follow up after three business days.
5. Invite interested owners or managers to purchase the Revenue Audit or take a 15-minute fit call.
6. Log replies, calls, purchases and objections.

Minimum activity target: 50 qualified contacts per week.

## Conversion path

1. Personalised observation or educational content
2. Property owner/manager reply
3. Three useful observations
4. Revenue Audit purchase
5. Delivery within five business days
6. Booking Sprint or Growth Partner upgrade
7. Testimonial and referral request
