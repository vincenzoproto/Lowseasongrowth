# Low Season Growth — Launch and Revenue Plan

_Last updated: 4 September 2026_

## Primary customer

Independent B&Bs, guest houses and small hospitality properties with identifiable low-season demand gaps and limited internal marketing capacity.

## Offer ladder

| Offer | Price | Primary job |
|---|---:|---|
| Opportunity Audit | €249 | Diagnose the biggest low-season commercial gaps |
| Booking Sprint | €479 | Build one offer, its messaging and a 30-day activation plan |
| Growth Partner | €990 | Build the full campaign and guide implementation for 30 days |

The Booking Sprint is the main offer. The Audit is the low-friction entry point. Growth Partner should normally be sold after a short fit conversation.

## Conversion path

1. Educational Instagram or LinkedIn content
2. Profile visit
3. Website or direct conversation
4. 15-minute fit call for qualified prospects
5. Booking Sprint or Growth Partner purchase
6. Delivery, testimonial and referral request

## Website priorities

- Keep one primary CTA: **Explore the Booking Sprint**
- Add a secondary CTA: **Book a 15-minute fit call**
- Add a real founder section with Vincenzo Proto's hospitality and commercial background
- Show a sample deliverable without revealing client-sensitive data
- Add an honest FAQ covering fit, timing, inputs, exclusions and refunds
- Add testimonials only after receiving explicit client permission
- Confirm refund, terms, privacy and digital-service fulfilment policies
- Remove the Shopify attribution from the public footer when the theme allows it
- Validate mobile layout and checkout before each theme publication

## Measurement

Track weekly:

- Qualified properties contacted
- Replies
- Calls booked
- Audit purchases
- Sprint purchases
- Growth Partner purchases
- Revenue
- Delivery capacity and turnaround time

Do not optimise for followers alone. The useful leading indicator is qualified conversations with property owners and managers.
