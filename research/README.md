# Low Season Growth Research

Low Season Growth publishes transparent, reproducible research on hotel seasonality, tourism demand and weak-date commercial planning for independent hotels, B&Bs and guesthouses.

Official research hub: https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/research

## Research Notes

### Research Note 01 — European Tourism Seasonality 2026

Focus: peak-vs-quiet-month concentration across selected European tourism markets.

- Live article: https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/european-tourism-seasonality-2026-lsg-research-note
- Methodology note: [european-tourism-seasonality-2026.md](./european-tourism-seasonality-2026.md)
- Data: [data/research-note-01-seasonality-gap.csv](./data/research-note-01-seasonality-gap.csv)
- Original LSG metric: **Peak-to-Trough Concentration Gap**
- DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22937580](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22937580)

### Research Note 02 — International Tourism Demand in Europe 2026

Focus: international vs domestic tourism demand growth in the EU during the first half of 2026.

- Live article: https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/international-tourism-demand-europe-2026-lsg-research-note
- Methodology note: [international-tourism-demand-europe-2026.md](./international-tourism-demand-europe-2026.md)
- Data: [data/research-note-02-international-demand.csv](./data/research-note-02-international-demand.csv)
- DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22938213](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22938213)

### Research Note 03 — Caribbean Low Season 2026

Focus: Caribbean low-season RevPAR and source-market diversification, using published CHTA / Amadeus figures.

- Live article: https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/caribbean-low-season-2026-revpar-south-american-demand
- Methodology note: [caribbean-low-season-demand-2026.md](./caribbean-low-season-demand-2026.md)
- Data: [data/research-note-03-caribbean-low-season.csv](./data/research-note-03-caribbean-low-season.csv)
- Source institutions: **Caribbean Hotel and Tourism Association (CHTA) and Amadeus**
- DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22938535](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22938535)

### Research Note 04 — Japan Tourism 2026

Focus: aggregate inbound cooling versus source-market divergence, using official JNTO and Japan Tourism Agency statistics.

- Live article: https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/japan-tourism-2026-source-market-divergence-lsg-research-note-04
- Methodology note: [japan-tourism-demand-2026.md](./japan-tourism-demand-2026.md)
- Data: [data/research-note-04-japan-demand.csv](./data/research-note-04-japan-demand.csv)
- Source institutions: **Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) and Japan Tourism Agency**
- DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22938971](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22938971)
