# 100-Property Low Season Benchmark 2026

Low Season Growth is recruiting up to 100 independent hospitality properties and professionals to contribute comparable real-world signals about weak-demand periods.

Official open call:
https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/100-property-low-season-benchmark-2026

## Important sampling note

**100 is the recruitment target. It is not a claim that 100 usable responses have already been collected.**

The final benchmark will report:
- achieved usable sample size;
- geography;
- contributor/property mix;
- response definitions;
- material limitations.

The findings will be descriptive and will not be presented as statistically representative of the entire global hotel industry.

## Standard questions

1. Which months or weeks are hardest to fill?
2. Which channel still produces bookings in those periods?
3. Is there one offer or action that has actually worked before?
4. Where do you feel the biggest booking loss happens?

## Response categories

### Booking channel
Suggested normalized categories:
- direct
- OTA
- repeat_guest
- agency_or_partner
- corporate_or_group
- social
- other

### Perceived booking-loss point
Suggested normalized categories:
- visibility
- positioning_or_offer
- price
- website_or_booking_conversion
- follow_up
- availability_or_product_fit
- other

## Contributor eligibility

A usable response must come from a hospitality property, short-stay operator, destination partner, revenue professional or hospitality sales professional with direct knowledge of low-season demand.

Duplicate responses from the same property may be consolidated.

## Attribution

Participants can choose:
- anonymous;
- credited organization/property;
- credited organization/property plus a short public quote.

Public attribution or quotes should be used only when the participant has explicitly chosen to be credited.

## Data handling

Aggregate findings may be reported without identifying individual properties. The final report should not disclose contact details or non-public commercial information.
