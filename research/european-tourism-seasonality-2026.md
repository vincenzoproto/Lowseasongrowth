## Purpose

This note documents the methodology and source references behind Low Season Growth's first public research note on European tourism seasonality.

It is intended to make the analysis transparent and reproducible. It is not an official Eurostat indicator, a forecast, or a benchmark for any individual hotel.

## LSG descriptive metric

**Peak-to-Trough Concentration Gap**

```
share of annual nights in the two busiest months
minus
share of annual nights in the two slowest months
```

The metric is a simple percentage-point difference calculated by Low Season Growth from Eurostat's published 2025 country table.

### Selected examples

| Country | 2 busiest months | 2 slowest months | LSG gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Croatia | 54.5% | 1.4% | 53.1 pp |
| France | 36.0% | 8.3% | 27.7 pp |
| Italy | 34.5% | 7.7% | 26.8 pp |
| Cyprus | 29.8% | 3.8% | 26.0 pp |
| Latvia | 29.1% | 10.3% | 18.8 pp |
| Lithuania | 24.5% | 13.2% | 11.3 pp |

## Interpretation

A larger gap indicates that national tourist-accommodation nights are more concentrated around the busiest months relative to the weakest months.

The metric does **not** indicate whether an individual hotel is performing well or badly. National accommodation statistics cover multiple accommodation types and do not replace property-level booking pace, occupancy, ADR, channel mix, or market analysis.

## Headline context

According to Eurostat:

- EU tourist accommodation recorded nearly 3.1 billion nights in 2025.
- 31.1% of 2025 EU nights were concentrated in July and August.
- August recorded roughly 3.6 times the tourism nights of January.
- EU accommodation nights reached 1.321 billion in the first half of 2026, up 1.7% year on year.
- Foreign visitors accounted for 48.9% of EU accommodation nights in the first half of 2026.
