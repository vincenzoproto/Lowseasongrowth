# LSG Global Low-Season Evidence Map 2026

**Low Season Growth** publishes source-backed descriptive research on hotel seasonality, tourism demand and weak-demand periods.

Official map page: https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/global-low-season-evidence-map-2026

> **Important:** the evidence below is intentionally not converted into a cross-country ranking. The underlying sources, periods and metrics differ. Each region should be read within its own source and methodology context.

## Europe

### Seasonality concentration — Research Note 01

**Source:** Eurostat tourist-accommodation statistics  
**Reference year:** 2025  
**Question:** How concentrated are annual accommodation nights between the two busiest and two slowest months?

Selected LSG Peak-to-Trough Concentration Gap examples:

### International vs domestic demand — Research Note 02

**Source:** Eurostat  
**Reference period:** H1 2026 / Q1 2026

- Foreign guest-night growth: **+2.5%**
- Domestic guest-night growth: **+0.9%**
- Foreign share of EU accommodation nights in H1 2026: **48.9%**
- LSG calculation: international guest-night growth was about **2.78×** domestic growth.
- LSG calculation: about **73.1%** of the additional Q1 nights came from international visitors.

- Research Note: https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/international-tourism-demand-europe-2026-lsg-research-note
- CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vincenzoproto/Lowseasongrowth/main/research/data/research-note-02-international-demand.csv

## Caribbean

### Low-season RevPAR and source-market diversification — Research Note 03

**Source:** Caribbean Hotel and Tourism Association (CHTA) and Amadeus  
**Source-data period:** April 2025–March 2026

- High-season hotel RevPAR: **US$183**
- Low-season hotel RevPAR: **US$125**
- End-of-year holiday RevPAR: **US$283**
- LSG calculation: low-season RevPAR was about **31.7% below** the reported high-season level.
- Latin American demand: **+24% YoY**
- Brazil low-season arrivals: **+60%**
- Colombia low-season arrivals: **+26%**

- Research Note: https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/caribbean-low-season-2026-revpar-south-american-demand
- CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vincenzoproto/Lowseasongrowth/main/research/data/research-note-03-caribbean-low-season.csv

## Japan

### Aggregate cooling, source-market divergence — Research Note 04

**Sources:** Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) and Japan Tourism Agency  
**Reference period:** June–August 2026

- August 2026 visitor arrivals: **3,098,900**
- August 2026 visitor arrivals YoY: **-9.6%**
- Source markets recording record August arrivals: **14**
- Italy and Spain recorded their highest monthly visitor arrivals on record.
- July room occupancy rate: **60.8%**
- June room occupancy rate: **57.2%**
- LSG calculation: July-to-August visitor arrivals fell about **10.0% month over month**.
- LSG calculation: foreign guest nights represented about **24.84%** of total guest nights in July.

- Research Note: https://lowseasongrowth.com/blogs/news/japan-tourism-2026-source-market-divergence-lsg-research-note-04
- CSV: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vincenzoproto/Lowseasongrowth/main/research/data/research-note-04-japan-demand.csv

## How to use this evidence

This map is designed for journalists, hotel operators, researchers and hospitality professionals who need a fast route into the underlying evidence.

It does **not** claim that:
- these regions have directly comparable low-season intensity;
- national or regional statistics represent every individual hotel;
- descriptive figures establish causality;
- LSG research notes are forecasts.
