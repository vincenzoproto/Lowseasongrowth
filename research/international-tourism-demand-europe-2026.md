## Purpose

This note documents the source figures and calculations behind Low Season Growth Research Note 02 on international and domestic tourism demand growth in Europe during the first half of 2026.

It is intended to make the analysis transparent and reproducible. It is descriptive analysis, not a forecast or a benchmark for an individual hotel.

## Headline figures

First half of 2026:

- Total EU tourist-accommodation nights: **1.321 billion**
- Total year-on-year growth: **+1.7%**
- Foreign visitor share of nights: **48.9%**
- Foreign visitor night growth: **+2.5%**
- Domestic visitor night growth: **+0.9%**

Simple LSG comparison:

```
2.5 / 0.9 = 2.78
```

So international guest nights grew about **2.8 times faster** than domestic guest nights in percentage terms during the first half of 2026.

## Q1 contribution calculation

Eurostat reported:

- additional international nights: **11.4 million**
- additional domestic nights: **4.2 million**

LSG descriptive calculation:

```
11.4 / (11.4 + 4.2) = 0.7308
```

So approximately **73.1%** of the additional nights recorded in Q1 came from international visitors.

## Interpretation

The figures indicate that international demand contributed disproportionately to recent EU tourism growth, but exposure to foreign demand varies substantially by country.

This does **not** imply that every destination or hotel should target international guests. Property-level strategy should consider actual source markets, booking windows, access, channel mix, pricing, positioning and operational fit.
