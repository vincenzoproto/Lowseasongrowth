# Low Season Growth Research Methodology

## Scope

Low Season Growth research focuses on hotel seasonality, tourism demand and commercial planning for independent hospitality businesses.

The research is designed to support interpretation and discussion. It is not property-level revenue management, investment advice or forecasting.

## Source hierarchy

LSG prioritises primary public sources, including:

1. Eurostat
2. national statistical authorities
3. official tourism bodies
4. clearly identified industry datasets when primary public data is unavailable

Each Research Note links to the original source material used.

## Separation of evidence and interpretation

LSG distinguishes between:

- **official source data** — figures published by the underlying institution;
- **LSG calculations** — arithmetic derived from cited source data;
- **commercial interpretation** — implications for hotel marketing, sales or planning.

LSG-created metrics are labelled explicitly and are not presented as official source indicators.

## Research Note 01 metric

### LSG Peak-to-Trough Concentration Gap

Formula:

```
share of annual accommodation nights in the two busiest months
minus
share of annual accommodation nights in the two slowest months
```

The result is expressed in percentage points.

A larger gap indicates stronger concentration around peak months relative to the weakest months.

It does not measure hotel profitability, occupancy quality, ADR performance or commercial execution.

## Research Note 02 calculations

### International-to-domestic growth ratio

```
foreign guest-night growth rate / domestic guest-night growth rate
```

Using Eurostat H1 2026 figures:

```
2.5 / 0.9 = 2.78
```

### International share of additional Q1 nights

```
additional international nights /
(additional international nights + additional domestic nights)
```

Using Eurostat figures:

```
11.4 / (11.4 + 4.2) = 73.1%
```

## Research Note 03 calculations

Research Note 03 uses figures published in the 2026 Caribbean Travel Trends Report from the Caribbean Hotel and Tourism Association (CHTA) and Amadeus.

### High-season vs low-season RevPAR gap

```
US$183 - US$125 = US$58
```

Low-season RevPAR below the reported high-season level:

```
58 / 183 = 31.7%
```

### Holiday vs low-season RevPAR gap

```
US$283 - US$125 = US$158
```

Low-season RevPAR below the reported year-end holiday level:

```
158 / 283 = 55.8%
```

These are Low Season Growth descriptive calculations from the published CHTA / Amadeus figures. They are not official CHTA or Amadeus indicators.

## Research Note 04 calculations

Research Note 04 uses official JNTO visitor-arrival figures and Japan Tourism Agency accommodation statistics.

### July-to-August inbound change

```
3,098,900 - 3,442,100 = -343,200 visitors
```

```
(3,098,900 / 3,442,100 - 1) × 100 = -9.97%
```

### Foreign share of guest nights

July 2026:

```
13.58 / 54.67 = 24.84%
```

June 2026:

```
12.22 / 45.82 = 26.67%
```

### Occupancy movement

```
60.8% - 57.2% = +3.6 percentage points
```

These are Low Season Growth descriptive calculations. They are not official JNTO or Japan Tourism Agency indicators.

Important methodological note: Japan's Accommodation Travel Statistics Survey changed its stratification basis from employee count to room count from January 2026. Year-on-year accommodation comparisons can therefore include effects from the survey redesign.

## Limitations

- National data does not represent every destination or hotel equally.
- Accommodation datasets may include multiple property types.
- Descriptive metrics do not explain causality.
- Commercial implications depend on local context.
- Source revisions can change historical figures.
- LSG Research Notes are not forecasts.

## Corrections

If a material calculation error or primary-source revision is identified, Low Season Growth may update the relevant Research Note and dataset. Public repository history provides a transparent record of revisions.
