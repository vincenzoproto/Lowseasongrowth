## Citation and interpretation

- Official source figures remain attributed to the original source institution.
- Low Season Growth calculations are explicitly labelled as LSG descriptive calculations.
- National and regional tourism statistics should not be treated as individual-hotel performance benchmarks.
- The public Research Notes are descriptive analyses, not forecasts.
