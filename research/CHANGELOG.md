# Low Season Growth Research Changelog

## 29 September 2026 — Research Note 02 regional evidence amendment

### Added
- A separately labelled annual 2024 regional domestic/international accommodation-demand supplement to Research Note 02, based on Eurostat's *Regions in Europe — 2026 edition*, published 23 September 2026.
- Published source figures: foreign nights exceeded domestic nights in 65 of 244 covered EU regions (26.6%); selected regional shares are identified explicitly as foreign or domestic.
- A practical same-month source-market check for independent hotels, with official evidence and LSG interpretation clearly separated.
- Limitations distinguishing annual 2024 regional shares from H1/Q1 2026 growth, booking patterns and property-level low-season demand.

### Updated
- Existing Shopify Research Note 02 in English and Italian, without creating another article.
- Existing Research Hub entry in English and Italian and the Global Evidence Map's English page.
- Working-repository Note 02, Research README and Global Evidence Map in Markdown and JSON. The JSON has a separate 2024 evidence entry, not additional values mixed into the 2026 growth entry.
- Visible amendment date and citation guidance for the live-page supplement.

### Preserved
- Research Note 01 and all Peak-to-Trough Concentration Gap values.
- Original 2026 source figures and calculations in Research Note 02.
- All original CSV datasets and their checksums; the new 2024 figures are not represented as part of the original 2026 CSV.
- Existing article/page titles, handles and initial publication dates.
- DOI deposits and frozen research-v1.0 / research-v1.1 snapshots. This is a working-page evidence extension, not a new archived data-package version.
