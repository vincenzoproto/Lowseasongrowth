# Zenodo deposit preparation — Low Season Growth Research

This directory prepares Low Season Growth Research Notes 01, 02, 03 and 04 for **separate Zenodo dataset deposits**, so each research object can receive its own DOI.

## Why separate deposits

Research Notes 01, 02 and 03 use different datasets, methods and citation titles. Separate Zenodo records make each output independently citable and easier to discover.

## DOI workflow

Zenodo assigns a DOI when a record is published. A DOI can also be reserved while the deposit is still a draft and then added back into the files before publication if desired.

After each DOI is created:
1. add the DOI to the corresponding live Research Note;
2. add it to `CITATION.cff`, `research/publications.bib`, `research/publications.json` and `research/datasets.jsonld`;
3. add it to the Shopify citation page and Research & Media page.
