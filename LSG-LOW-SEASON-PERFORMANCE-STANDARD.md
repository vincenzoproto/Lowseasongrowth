# LSG Low Season Performance Standard — Development Programme

Low Season Growth is developing a proprietary standard for assessing how independent hotels identify, diagnose, activate and measure action around weak dates.

## Current status

**Development programme — not an accredited certification scheme.**

No hotel is currently listed as accredited or certified under an accredited LSG scheme.

## What the draft standard is designed to assess

1. Demand Intelligence
2. Diagnosis
3. Distribution & Conversion
4. Commercial Activation
5. Measurement & Learning

The intended future architecture separates scheme ownership from the certification decision. Low Season Growth would own the standard and methodology; a mature certification decision would be made through an independent third-party certification process.

## Why this exists

A weak date is not a single marketing problem. A hotel may face insufficient demand, poor market fit, weak distribution visibility, booking-path friction, or an activation problem. The standard is designed to make those distinctions auditable and evidence-based.

## Development principles

- evidence before claims;
- diagnosis before action;
- failures remain part of the evidence base;
- software signals do not automatically equal conformity;
- consulting does not guarantee certification;
- public status must be verifiable;
- accreditation language is not used before it is factually applicable.
