# Low Season Growth

**Low Season Growth (LSG)** is a hospitality commercial-strategy and research project focused on helping independent hotels, B&Bs and guesthouses understand and improve weak-demand periods.

Official website: https://lowseasongrowth.com

## What Low Season Growth focuses on

LSG works on practical questions around:

- hotel low-season and shoulder-season demand
- weak-date diagnosis before discounting
- direct-booking strategy for independent hotels
- hotel sales and proactive demand generation
- booking-window and source-market interpretation
- offer design, distribution and conversion
- hospitality research, public datasets and decision-support tools

The central idea is simple: **not every weak hotel date has the same cause**. A soft period can reflect demand, market fit, distribution or conversion issues, and the commercial response should match the underlying problem.

## Research

The `research/` directory contains Low Season Growth public research notes, datasets, evidence maps and citation metadata.

LSG research is designed to keep public-source data, descriptive calculations and commercial interpretation clearly separated. Where original LSG metrics are used, they are identified as such and are not presented as official indicators from the underlying source institution.

## LSG Intelligence

**LSG Intelligence** is the beta decision-support layer being developed by Low Season Growth for hotel weak dates.

The public-facing beta explains the problem framework and decision logic. Internal models, learning systems, CRM data and operational intelligence remain private.

Public beta: https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/lsg-intelligence

## Public repository scope

This repository is intentionally limited to public-facing material:

- research and citation metadata
- press and media assets
- brand assets intended for public use
- machine-readable organisation metadata
- public AI/knowledge documentation

Operational CRM data, prospect lists, customer data, sales-partner records, private procedures, Shopify backups and internal workflows are not stored in this public repository.
