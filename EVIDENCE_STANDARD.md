# LSG Evidence Standard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Published:** 2 October 2026  
**Canonical page:** https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/lsg-evidence-standard

The LSG Evidence Standard defines how Low Season Growth labels commercial evidence from hotel weak-date work.

## E0 — Hypothesis / assumption

A planning assumption or commercial hypothesis before a result is observed. E0 is not outcome evidence.

## E1 — Observed commercial signal

An intermediate response such as:
- qualified reply;
- direct enquiry;
- proposal request;
- booking attempt;
- reactivated past guest.

E1 is not a booking and not revenue.

## E2 — Confirmed booking

A reservation has been confirmed and can be reconciled to the relevant property and stay period. It may still cancel or fail to complete.

## E3 — Completed stay

The stay has completed and completed room nights can be reconciled. Realized room revenue may be reported when available and authorized.

## E4 — Mature case evidence

The measurement window is mature; relevant cancellations and completed stays have been reconciled; material confounding changes and limitations are documented; and a named public version has property approval.

E4 does not automatically establish causal incremental lift.

## E5 — Incremental / causal evidence

A result supported by a credible comparison design such as a control, holdout, randomized test, matched comparison or another defensible counterfactual.

## Attribution confidence

Separate from evidence level:

- **low** — temporal association or weak tracking;
- **medium** — traceable campaign / sales / booking path with known limitations;
- **high** — reconciled identifiers and a clear attribution rule.

Attribution remains distinct from causation.

## Reporting rules

- Do not report E1 as bookings.
- Do not report E2 as realized stays or realized revenue.
- Do not report E3/E4 as incremental lift without E5-level design.
- State measurement dates for provisional outcomes.
- Record cancellations and material commercial changes.
- Keep failed and inconclusive tests in the private learning dataset.
- Named public case studies require property approval.
