# LSG Low Season Performance Standard — Public Consultation Round 1

**Status:** Development Programme  
**Consultation round:** 1  
**Opened:** 2 October 2026  
**Initial comment period:** through 30 November 2026

Low Season Growth is developing a proprietary conformity-assessment scheme for the hospitality sector.

The proposed certification object is the hotel process used to identify, diagnose, activate and measure commercial action around weak-demand dates.

This is **not currently an accredited certification scheme** and no accredited LSG certificates are being issued.

## Who we are consulting

We welcome technical comments from:
- independent hotel owners and General Managers;
- revenue-management and commercial professionals;
- distribution, PMS and channel-management specialists;
- hotel and tourism associations;
- Certification Bodies;
- conformity-assessment and accreditation specialists;
- hospitality academics and researchers.

## Consultation questions

1. Does the proposed certification object address a recognisable problem in hotel operations?
2. Are the five domains understandable and auditable?
3. Which requirements would be too subjective to support third-party certification?
4. What evidence should a hotel be expected to provide without unnecessary access to confidential guest or commercial data?
5. What is missing from the proposed scope?
6. Are there existing standards or schemes that LSG should explicitly avoid duplicating?
7. Would independent third-party certification create useful trust for hotel owners, groups, partners or other stakeholders?
8. What conditions would make such a certificate credible in practice?

## Current five-domain structure

- Demand Intelligence
- Diagnosis
- Distribution & Conversion
- Commercial Activation
- Measurement & Learning

## How comments are handled

Material comments are recorded in a controlled consultation log.

For each substantive comment, the Scheme Owner records whether the proposal is:
- accepted;
- partially accepted; or
- not accepted,

together with the rationale and any resulting change to the controlled standard.

Silence is never treated as support.

## Independence

Participation in consultation does not imply:
- endorsement of Low Season Growth;
- endorsement of the final scheme;
- certification;
- accreditation;
- commercial commitment.

The intended mature model separates Low Season Growth as Scheme Owner from the independent Certification Body responsible for evaluation, review and certification decision.
