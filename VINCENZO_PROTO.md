# Vincenzo Proto — Hospitality Research & Commercial Strategy

**Founder, Low Season Growth**

Canonical profile: https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/vincenzo-proto  
Company about: https://lowseasongrowth.com/pages/about  
Medium: https://medium.com/@digitalvincenzoproto

Vincenzo Proto is the founder of **Low Season Growth (LSG)**, a hospitality commercial-strategy and research initiative focused on weak-demand periods for independent hotels, B&Bs and guesthouses.

His work combines:
- hotel weak-date diagnosis;
- low-season commercial strategy;
- direct-booking strategy;
- B2B hotel sales and proactive demand generation;
- tourism seasonality and source-market analysis;
- public hospitality research and decision-support methods.

## Background

His professional background combines business economics, B2B sales and hospitality experience, including hotel administration at Club Med in St Moritz.

LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/vincenzoproto
